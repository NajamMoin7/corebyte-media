# PRODUCT.md — Core Byte Media

**Register:** brand (this is a marketing/agency site; the design IS the product).

## What it is
Marketing site for Core Byte Media, a studio offering three services under one
roof: **graphic design**, **web development**, and **agentic AI** (chatbots,
copilots, autonomous agents). Multi-page Next.js site, heavy on real-time 3D
(WebGL) and motion, with a live agentic chatbot embedded as proof of the AI work.

## Users / audience
Prospective clients evaluating an agency: startup founders, marketing leads, and
product owners who want design + build + AI from one partner. They are
design-conscious and skeptical of generic "AI agency" sites. They decide on the
strength of the work and the craft of the site itself.

## Brand
- **Mark:** a gold hexagon enclosing a joined "CB" monogram.
- **Feeling:** engineering precision meets craft. Confident, technical, alive.
- **Color DNA:** gold on near-black. This is the differentiator: most AI-agency
  sites default to purple/blue. We do not.
- **Voice:** plain, concrete, a little wry. We say what we build and what it does.
  No "elevate / seamless / unleash / next-gen". No em-dashes.

## Anti-references (do NOT look like these)
- Purple-gradient "AI startup" templates.
- Centered-hero-over-dark-mesh with three identical feature cards.
- Generic glassmorphism on everything.
- Stocky corporate-teal SaaS.
- Beige/brass "premium craft" palette (the gold is an accent on black, not a
  background wash).

## Strategic principles
1. **Show, don't claim.** The site's own 3D, motion, and live chatbot ARE the
   portfolio. Craft is the argument.
2. **One accent, locked.** Gold everywhere, no stray colors.
3. **Dark, always.** One theme across all pages, no mid-scroll inversions.
4. **Motion must be motivated.** Every animation communicates hierarchy,
   sequence, feedback, or state. No motion for show.
5. **Mobile is first-class.** Every WebGL/scroll moment has a real mobile fallback.
6. **Accessible.** WCAG AA contrast, reduced-motion honored, keyboard-navigable.

## Success criteria
- Feels like an award-tier studio site, not an AI template.
- Loads fast (LCP < 2.5s), CLS < 0.1, motion smooth on mid-range mobile.
- The live chatbot answers real questions about Core Byte Media.
- A visitor can understand the three services and reach one clear CTA: **Start a project**.
